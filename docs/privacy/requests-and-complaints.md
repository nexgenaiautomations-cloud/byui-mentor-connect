## How a request reaches us

Two published routes, both listed in the privacy notice at `/privacy`:

1. Directly to the program owner at `GabrielD@nexgenaiintegrations.com`
2. Through the BYUI CAN head admin, who forwards it

BYU-Idaho may also route a request to us as the data controller. Those are
handled on the same timeline and the head admin is kept on the thread.

## Handling steps

1. **Acknowledge** within 2 business days, in writing, to whoever raised it.
2. **Verify** the requester actually owns the account before anything is
   disclosed, changed, or deleted. For a request arriving through BYU-Idaho,
   the institution's confirmation is sufficient verification.
3. **Classify** it:
   - *Individual-rights request* — access, correction, deletion, objection,
     restriction, or a copy of the data. Continue below.
   - *Complaint about a practice* — answer with what the system actually does,
     and if the complaint reveals a real gap, it becomes a change with a named
     owner and a fix date under the privacy review checklist.
   - *Possible data incident* — stop and switch to the incident response
     runbook. Notification clocks there take precedence over this document.
4. **Resolve** within 14 days of verification. If more time is genuinely
   needed, say so before day 14 with a reason and a date.
5. **Record** the request, what was decided, and the date in the same place
   incidents are logged, so the history survives.

## What we can do for each right

| Right | How it is satisfied |
|---|---|
| Access / know what is held | The account holder sees their own profile in app settings; on request we describe every field held about them |
| Correction / rectification | Self-serve in app settings at any time |
| Deletion / erasure | Verified request, head-admin approval, documented deletion script, confirmed to the requester within 14 days. Audit history is retained in anonymized form for accountability — this is stated in the privacy notice, not a silent exception |
| Objection / restriction | Handled manually — an account can be deactivated, or a specific field restricted to admins, while the underlying question is resolved |
| Portability | No self-serve export exists today. A copy of an account's data is produced manually on request, in a machine-readable format |
| Do not sell / do not share | Nothing to action: the data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train models. Stated in the privacy notice |

## Escalation

Anything that looks like it may be a reportable breach, involves
FERPA-covered records, or that we cannot resolve to the requester's
satisfaction goes to the BYUI CAN head admin and, where warranted, to
BYU-Idaho IT Security. We escalate early rather than late.

## Review

Re-reviewed annually alongside the privacy notice, or whenever the rights
we can support change.
